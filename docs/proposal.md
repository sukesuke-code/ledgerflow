# LedgerFlow

_Tokenized invoice financing for B2B cross-border trade on Solana_

## Summary

LedgerFlow lets enterprises mint their unpaid invoices as on-chain tokens and get instant stablecoin financing from a liquidity pool, bypassing slow bank factoring. Companies get working capital in minutes while investors earn yield backed by real receivables.

## Target users

SMEs and mid-size enterprises needing working capital, plus yield-seeking stablecoin investors

## Problem

Traditional invoice factoring is slow, opaque, and expensive, especially for cross-border B2B trade.

## Solution

Tokenize verified invoices as NFTs/RWA and let a permissionless lending pool instantly advance stablecoins against them, with automated repayment on invoice settlement.

## MVP features

- Invoice upload & on-chain verification via oracle/KYB partner
- Mint invoice as RWA token representing receivable
- Instant stablecoin advance from liquidity pool at risk-based rate
- Automated repayment & yield distribution when invoice is paid
- Dashboard for enterprises and liquidity providers

## Chains

Solana

## Tech

Anchor, Solana Program Library, USDC, Chainlink/Pyth oracle, Next.js, Supabase

## Category

DeFi

## Why now

Stablecoin infrastructure and RWA tooling on Solana have matured enough to make real-world invoice settlement fast and cheap, while enterprises face tighter credit from traditional banks.

## Roadmap

- Partner with a KYB/credit-scoring provider for invoice verification
- Pilot with 5-10 SMEs in a specific trade corridor
- Add secondary market for trading invoice tokens
